Privacy policy for Bismarck

Effective from 24 October 2026

Bismarck is a card game by Alcode AB. This policy describes how the app handles information about you.

We collect no data

The app does not collect, transmit or share any information about you or your device. It does not use the internet, shows no advertising and contains no third-party analytics or tracking services.

What is stored on your phone

For the game to work, the following is stored on your device only:

  • the profile names you enter,
  • your played games, statistics, league results and achievements,
  • settings, and a game in progress so that it can be continued.

This information never leaves the device and Alcode AB has no access to it. You remove it by deleting a profile in the game or by uninstalling the app. If backup is turned on on your phone, the operating system may include the app's files in your own backup; this is controlled by your phone's settings.

Children

As the app collects no data, no data about children is collected either.

Changes

If the app changes so that it handles information differently, this policy will be updated here, with a new date.
